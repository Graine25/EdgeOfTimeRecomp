REEOT_DESC_FULLSCREEN	Fills the display, or runs in a window that can be moved and resized.
REEOT_DESC_RESOLUTION	The resolution the game renders at in fullscreen. Native is the console's own 1120x632, scaled by Render Scale; Display is the display's own, scaled by Display Scale. In a window the game renders at the height the display suggests and the picture stretches to the window.
REEOT_DESC_ASPECT	The shape the game builds its picture for in fullscreen. Wider than 16:9 shows more to the sides rather than stretching. In a window the picture follows the window.
REEOT_DESC_FPS_LIMIT	The most frames per second the game will run at. The game runs one step per frame, so this paces everything, not just the display.
REEOT_DESC_VSYNC	Waits for the display before showing a frame, which stops tearing and caps the rate at the display's.
REEOT_DESC_FPS_OVERLAY	Shows the frame rate, the frame times, and where the GPU's time goes pass by pass over the last 30 frames, with the draw count and the game thread's share. For finding what a view costs.
REEOT_DESC_RENDER_SCALE	How much larger or smaller than the console's own resolution to render, when Resolution is Native.
REEOT_DESC_PIP_SCALE	The resolution of the picture-in-picture window the abilities open, as a share of the main picture's. It is a second scene with its own lights and effects; at 50% it costs a quarter of the pixels. Takes effect on restart.
REEOT_DESC_BRIGHTNESS	Lifts or lowers the whole picture. Applied at the end, on top of the game's own display curve.
REEOT_DESC_CONTRAST	Stretches or flattens the picture around its middle grey.
REEOT_DESC_SATURATION	How strong the colours are: 0% is greyscale, 100% is the game's own.
REEOT_DESC_GAMMA	The curve between dark and light, before the console's own ramp.
REEOT_DESC_PRESET	Sets Anti-Aliasing, Anisotropic Filtering, Shadow Resolution and the Upscale Filter together. Changing any of those by hand makes this Custom.
REEOT_DESC_MSAA	Smooths the edges of geometry by drawing every scene pass with several samples per pixel. Costs memory and bandwidth in proportion.
REEOT_DESC_TAA	Blends each frame with the previous one, offset by a fraction of a pixel and reprojected through the camera, so distant edges stop shimmering. Works with or without multisampling.
REEOT_DESC_ANISOTROPY	Keeps floors and walls sharp at grazing angles. Near free on a modern card.
REEOT_DESC_SHADOW_SIZE	The size of each shadow map. Auto follows the render resolution; the fixed sizes trade sharpness for memory.
REEOT_DESC_SHADOW_DISTANCE	How far shadows are cast, as a multiple of what the level asks for. Farther spreads the same map over more ground.
REEOT_DESC_UPSCALE	The filter used to fit the rendered image to the window. Lanczos is the sharpest, bilinear the softest.
REEOT_DESC_FOV	Widens or narrows the camera. 100% is the game's own.
REEOT_DESC_BLOOM	The glow around bright light. Off keeps the game's tone curve.
REEOT_DESC_DOF	Blurs what the camera is not focused on, including the shallow focus cutscenes use.
REEOT_DESC_MOTION_BLUR	Streaks fast movement, of the camera and of what it sees.
REEOT_DESC_RADIAL_BLUR	The speed streaks of free falls and dashes.
REEOT_DESC_HEAT	Heat vision and the shimmer of hot air.
REEOT_DESC_FILM_GRAIN	A fine grain over the whole picture.
REEOT_DESC_HALO	Light bleeding from the brightest parts of the picture.
REEOT_DESC_COLOR_GRADING	The game's own colour grade. Off shows the ungraded image.
